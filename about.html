About Northline

A studio built to be the team we couldn't hire

Northline Studio started with a simple frustration: businesses here could buy a logo in one place, ads in another and advice in a third, and none of the three ever talked to each other.

So we built the studio we wished existed. Design and marketing at the same desk, strategy before spend, and reporting a business owner can read at 11pm on a phone. Founder-led, deliberately small, and picky about the work we take on, and built for growing businesses across Kenya.

The name is the promise: a north line is the line you check your bearings against. Design that points north: every choice measured against where your business is actually trying to go.
