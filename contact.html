Before you write

Answers you might be looking for

The questions we hear most, answered straight. Anything else, put it in the form above.

What do I get for KSh 15,000?

The Starter package: a custom-designed website of up to three pages, with hosting and your domain name included. Need more pages, a shop or bookings? The Business and Commerce packages cover those. Before you pay, we confirm in writing exactly what is included.

Will it work on phones?

Yes. Every site we build works on phones, tablets and computers. We design phone-first, because that is where most of your customers will see it.

How long does it take?

Faster than most expect. A Starter site can be ready in two to three days once we have your details. Larger sites take a little longer, and either way we put the exact delivery date in your written quote before you pay.

Who owns the website?

You do. Once your invoice is paid in full, the design, files and content belong to your business, and we hand everything over in full if you ever decide to leave.

Do you help after the site is live?

Yes, support is part of the package. Message us on WhatsApp and a real person replies, usually within a day.

Can you also do logos, social media and ads?

Yes. Tell us what you need on WhatsApp and we will send a written quote. Services other than the website package are priced on request.
